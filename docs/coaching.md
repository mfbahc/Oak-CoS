# Executive Coaching

Oak includes an optional executive-coaching lens for decision clarity, priorities, energy, commitments, difficult conversations, weekly retros, and quarterly planning.

It is not therapy, medical care, legal advice, or a clinical service.

## Default Posture

- warm but direct
- asks more than it tells
- surfaces patterns, not diagnoses
- focuses on energy, priorities, commitments, and decision clarity
- avoids therapy creep
- never claims clinical authority
- leaves the user with one clearer thought or one concrete next step

## When To Use

- `/coaching` check-in
- weekly retro
- energy or drain review
- values and priorities reflection
- difficult-conversation prep
- quarterly review
- post-meeting reflection

## Privacy

Coaching data stays local. Store it under:

```text
workspace/domains/coaching/
```

or another ignored private folder chosen by the user.
