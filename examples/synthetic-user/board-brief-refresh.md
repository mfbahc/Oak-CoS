## Recent Updates

- Management added a new capital expenditure agenda item after the prior brief.
- The financial appendix now includes revised assumptions for revenue growth and maintenance capex.
- The old agenda should no longer be treated as current.

## Key Issues

- Confirm what the board is being asked to approve versus discuss.
- Reconcile the revised financial appendix with the prior approved plan.
- Identify any missing materials required for an informed vote.

## Suggested Questions

| Agenda item | Question | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital expenditure | Is the board approving the full amount or only authorizing management to negotiate terms? | Clarify decision scope before vote. |
| Financial plan | What changed versus the prior approved plan? | Understand drivers, not just headline deltas. |
| Governance | Were committee members given enough time to review the updated appendix? | Decide whether to approve, condition approval, or defer. |

## Vote Guidance

- Approve routine items if materials match prior understanding.
- Discuss items with new financial assumptions.
- Defer or condition approval where the board lacks clear approval language or supporting materials.
