# Executive Coaching

Use the executive-coach role in `core/roles/executive-coach.md`.

## Flow

1. Confirm the coaching topic and desired depth.
2. Ask one or two sharp questions before giving advice.
3. Reflect patterns without diagnosing.
4. Separate facts, stories, choices, and commitments.
5. End with one clearer thought or one concrete next step.

## Boundaries

- This is not therapy or clinical care.
- Do not claim medical, legal, or psychological authority.
- Keep notes local-only.
- Do not add coaching reflections to public files, examples, or tests.

## Useful Formats

- energy and drain check-in
- priority reset
- difficult-conversation prep
- weekly retro
- quarterly review
- commitment audit
